# Brand graphic exports


The SVG sources compose the approved outlined logo, mascot and motif; PNGs are rasterized from those sources. Typography is outlined Manrope under the included SIL OFL license.


- Open Graph: 1200 × 630. Preserve the whole frame; do not crop its wordmark or headline.

- Social square: 1080 × 1080. Keep at least 48 px around content; use this version for square feeds.

- Header: 1500 × 500. Decorative edges may crop by 30 px. For materially different platform aspect ratios, recompose the SVG instead of centering an automatic crop through the wordmark.

- Profile: 1024 × 1024 opaque symbol artwork. Circular or rounded platform masks may be applied; do not add corners to the source.

- Maskable: 512 × 512 opaque artwork, with the symbol inside the central safe circle. The web manifest explicitly distinguishes this from regular icons. It does not promise offline behavior.


The product targets Windows x64. Native ICO assets are included in the app; iOS and Android store submission layers are not claimed as application deliverables. The profile SVG is a reusable opaque platform source.


Sharing metadata uses local relative artwork paths. Configure absolute image and canonical URLs only after a real hosting destination is chosen.
