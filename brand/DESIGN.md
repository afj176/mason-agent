## Direction

Two offset courses form an M: construction with visible joins. Functional density, clear grouping and a strong reading axis

Offset M courses become Mortar stepped cheek planes and paper overlaps; companions vary head anatomy. The mascot is an original character, not a literal logo export. Mortar leads a completed five-identity avatar pack. Companion silhouettes vary while preserving the contract's material and color grammar.

## Color and type

Semantic roles are authoritative in tokens.json and tokens.css. Light backgrounds use #F8F6F2; dark backgrounds use #171A2B. Light actions use #4849A8; dark actions use #C3C2FF. Do not flatten the dark palette into the light palette. Preserve system high-contrast behavior in the native application.

Manrope is supplied under SIL OFL 1.1 in logo-kit/fonts. Native application controls use Windows system fonts. The logo wordmarks are outlined SVGs; do not recreate their spacing with live text.

## Usage

Use the primary, secondary, symbol, monochrome and reversed roles from logo-kit; its BRAND-GUIDE.md defines clear space, minimum sizes and misuse rules. Preserve the outlined wordmark. graphics/USAGE.md documents social crops, maskable safe areas and export dimensions. The mark, mascot and motif carry the identity without continuous motion.
